About HackMate

Many students want to join hackathons but cannot find enough teammates. Some have strong skills and no team. Some teams are one person short. Others have an idea and need different skills to build it.

HackMate connects students across colleges and cities so that nobody misses a hackathon simply because they could not find a team. Don’t Build Alone. Build Together.
